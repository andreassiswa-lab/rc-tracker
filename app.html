Real Revenue is recognised on each invoice's issue date — a project billed in instalments is counted in the phase each instalment was invoiced in. Potential (active enquiries + uninvoiced balance) sits in its delivery-date phase, as the best estimate of when it will be billed. Royalty is always priced at the delivery-date phase rate (royalty falls due at handover, Cl. 6.4) and is shown in the same row as the revenue that generated it — so Royalty ÷ Real Revenue in a row need not equal that phase's rate. Shortfall vs RC carries forward cumulatively (Cl. 5.3): Revenue Commitment = Base RC + Shortfall In. Amounts in AUD. Rates: HQ product 7%/11%/15% by phase, Non-HQ product & service 5% (Cl. 6); shipping excluded.
